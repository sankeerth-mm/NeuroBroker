# Research Gap to Implementation Mapping

This document provides a comprehensive mapping between the theoretical and algorithmic limitations identified in the distributed / federated learning literature and the engineering solutions implemented in **NeuroBroker**.

> [!NOTE]
> As specified in the design requirements, these research limitations are **addressed within the scope of the proposed prototype**, providing a practical, explainable, and benchmark-validated testbed.

---

## Comprehensive Research Mapping Table

|---|---|---|---|

| 3 | **Heterogeneous Hardware Imbalance**: Fast workers wait indefinitely for slow CPU workers (straggler bottleneck). | **Multi-Objective Resource Scorer**: Normalizes CPU cores, GPU compute capability, and memory to assign optimal partitions. | [`backend/app/scheduler/resource_score_scheduler.py`](file:///Users/sankeerth/Desktop/nb/backend/app/scheduler/resource_score_scheduler.py) |
